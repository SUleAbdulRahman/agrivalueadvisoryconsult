Finding the Missing Cost Base in an Oil Palm Business

A rapid systems diagnostic for a registered oil palm producer and processor in the Eastern Region of Ghana. Delivered at no charge as a pilot.

Client: oil palm processor, Eastern Region (name withheld at client’s request)Service: Agri-Performance Diagnostic™ (rapid snapshot)Scope: interview and document review

The client

The client is a registered Ghanaian agribusiness built on a zero-waste oil palm model: crude palm oil, refined palm oil, palm kernel oil and palm kernel cake from one fruit stream. The company is registered, licensed and banked, and targets institutional buyers such as schools, hospitals and wholesalers.

The commercial foundation is real. The question was whether the business had the management system to turn that foundation into profit.

How we worked

  1. 01
    Structured interviewA structured stakeholder interview with the founder, using our internal interview guide.
  2. 02
    Document reviewA line-by-line review of the company registration profile and business plan, and of how the two documents compare.
  3. 03
    Maturity scoringSix management areas scored on a five-point scale: cost tracking, production records, governance, market and contracts, compliance and quality, and funding readiness.
  4. 04
    Root cause and roadmapEvery finding traced to its cause, then a sequenced 90-day roadmap with owners and evidence of completion.
Scope note

This pilot was a desk-based review of client documents plus one structured interview with the founder. It did not include a site visit, mill inspection or review of transaction records. The findings below are documentary and interview-based, not verified site findings.

What we found

The diagnostic produced twenty-one findings. The most important:

  • Price was set before cost was known. On the business plan's own numbers, planned revenue did not cover planned variable cost in two of the first three cycles.
  • No production record. There was no fruit intake log, no oil output log and no oil extraction rate, the single most important efficiency measure in palm oil processing.
  • Numbers that did not reconcile. Stated annual throughput did not match the raw material budget, and the company profile and business plan described the business at two different stages.
  • Unmanaged supply risk. The mill depends on outgrowers for its fruit, but no outgrower supply agreements were in place.

"Twenty-one findings, one cause: there was no routine by which what happens in the field and the mill is written down, converted into a cost, and used to make the next decision."

This is the condition we call Prosperity Suffocation: an operation that is genuinely producing and selling, yet cannot convert that activity into retained profit, because nobody in the business can say what anything costs. Training does not resolve it. Additional capital does not resolve it. A system does.

What we recommended

Eight recommendations, ranked by return on effort and sequenced over 90 days. The first three need no capital:

  • A unit costing sheet converting fruit, fuel, electricity, labour, packaging and transport into a cost per litre and per drum.
  • A daily fruit and oil output log, from which extraction rate is calculated every week.
  • Cost-plus-margin pricing, so every price is traceable to a cost line.

The roadmap then moves to energy metering and tariff review, written outgrower supply agreements, monthly management accounts, food safety and quality documentation for institutional buyers, and a reconciled business plan ready for funders.

The client's view

"AVAC changed the way I think about pricing and profitability. I learned that I shouldn’t set the price of my palm oil before knowing the true cost of production. By calculating the actual cost per litre or gallon after production, I can now price strategically, protect my margins, and clearly understand my break-even point. AVAC also helped me strengthen my grant proposal, giving my business a stronger foundation to pursue funding and grow."

Founder and CEO, oil palm processor, Eastern Region

Status

The diagnostic report was delivered in August 2026. Implementation results have not yet been measured.
