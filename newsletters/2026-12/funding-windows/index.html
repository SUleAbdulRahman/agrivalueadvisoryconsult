Funding Windows: what is coming, and who it is really for

No major call is confirmed open this month. Here is what is coming, with dates, and the three documents to prepare while you wait.

AgriValue Advisory ConsultDecember 2026 edition4 min read

An honest picture this month: as of 21 November 2026 we could not confirm any major grant or concessional call that is open now to Ghanaian agribusinesses and cooperatives. Below are the windows we can confirm are coming, what each funds, and who it is really for. Use the quiet period to get your papers ready.

Coming: Common Fund for Commodities, 30th Call for Proposals

  • Status: the 29th call closed on 1 October 2026. The CFC says the 30th call "shall be announced soon". No date has been published.
  • What it funds: commodity value chain projects, mainly through loans and other debt instruments, up to USD 1.5 million for regular projects and up to USD 300,000 under its Fast-Track procedure.
  • Who: agripreneurs, SMEs and cooperatives, for projects in CFC member states. Start-ups are not financed, and a track record of at least three years is required.
  • Check first: that Ghana is a CFC member state for your project, and that you can carry a loan.

Coming: FCI4Africa Open Call 2 (EU-funded)

  • Dates: opens 1 April 2027, closes 31 May 2027 (as listed on the call page).
  • What it funds: up to EUR 50,000 per organisation, EUR 200,000 in total.
  • Who: innovation hubs that will deliver online training to the programme's existing projects. It is not a grant for farms or cooperatives.

Results pending: GAFSP 9th Call

  • Status: closed on 15 September 2026. Successful proposals are expected to be announced in February 2027.
  • Who: national governments only. Farmer organisations, businesses and NGOs cannot apply directly. If Ghana's proposal succeeds, the opportunity for producer groups will come through the government programme that follows.

Watch: 2027 Budget finance schemes

The 2027 Budget says the government will work with Development Bank Ghana, Ghana Exim Bank and participating banks on credit guarantees, equipment leasing, export financing and long-term capital. No application rules have been published yet. See our article Ghana's 2027 Budget: what it means for agribusinesses.

Be careful with "guaranteed grant" offers

If someone asks you to pay to be put forward for a grant, ask for the funder's official call page. Real calls are published by the funder, with dates and eligibility.

Use this month to prepare

  1. 01
    Registration documentsCertificate of registration, tax identification and a business bank account in the organisation's name.
  2. 02
    Twelve months of income and costsIn one table, month by month. Most applications ask for it.
  3. 03
    A one-page profileWhat you do, where, how many members or staff, what you produce and sell, and what you need the money for.
